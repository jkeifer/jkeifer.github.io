An introduction to the SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog (STAC) family of
standards—geospatial metadata, catalog organization, and search APIs—through
the lens of Element 84's EarthSearch: a STAC catalog of the complete Sentinel-2
archive in Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF, available via AWS's Registry of Open Data.
Covers the EarthSearch architecture, processing workflows built on cirrus-geo,
indexing and search with stac-server, and a demo querying EarthSearch with
open-source STAC tooling.
